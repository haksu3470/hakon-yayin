TO BE PUT INTO USE

 

1.      LOOK TO THE KTS`S MOUNTING AND MOUNT IT ON A WALL OR CONTROL PANEL.

2.      DOOR PLUG COMING FROM SHAFT`S OUTPUT PULL OUT FROM PANEL TERMINAL(120).CONNECT IT ON A2 TERMINAL AT KTS. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

3.      CONNECT A1 TERMINAL ON THE KTS TO THE  STOP TURNING ON THE CONTROL PANEL(120)  (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

4.      PULL UP THE DOOR CONTACT SIGNAL TURNING FROM SHAFT FROM CONTROL PANEL TERMINAL. (130) KTS CONNECT A3 TERMINAL. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

5.      CONNECT A4  TERMINAL ON THE KTS PLUG TURNING ON THE CONTROL PANEL.(130) (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

6.      PULL OUT FLOOR  SIGNAL OUTPUT COMING FROM THE SHAFT(140) FROM CONTROL PANEL TERMINAL CONNECT IT A6 TERMINAL ON KTS. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

7.      CONNECT A5 TERMINAL ON KTS TO THE FLOOR SIGNAL ON THE CONTROL PANEL.(140)       (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

8.      FLOOR SIGNAL COMING FROM THE SHAFT (JF-142) TURNING FROM PANEL TERMINAL (142) PULL OUT AND CONNECT IT TO  A7TERMINAL. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

9.      CONNECT A8 TERMINAL ON KTS TO FLOOR SIGNAL TURNING ON THE CONTROL PANEL(142) (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

10.  AT CONTROL PANEL`S PHASE`S(R)FUSE OUTPUT PUT OUT ALL CONNECTION.CONNECT IT TO TRD SIGNED TERMINAL.OUTPUT OF FUSE IN CONTROL PHASE ADDED UNDER THE TRG

        TERMINAL. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

11.  CONNECT TRG AND TRD TERMINALS  TO A9-A10 TERMINALS ON KTS. (LOOK PROJECT PAGE 2/2 )

12.  IF CONTROLS PANEL`S SUPPLY TRANSFORMER 380V AC THE TERMINALS YOU ADDED SHOULD BE 4 PEACES.CONNECT TRD,TRG TERMINALS TO A9 AND A10 TERMINALS ON KTS,TSG AND TRD TERMINALS TO B1,B2 TERMINALS. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

13.  REVISION BUTTON ON THE CONTROL PANEL(869)TERMINAL AND B4 TERMINAL ON KTS CONNECT THEM PARALLEL. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE) UPPER LIMIT SWITCH ON THE CONTROL PANEL KSR2 TURNING(818) TERMINAL AND B5 TERMINAL ON KTS CONNECT PARALLEL EACH OTHER . (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE). LOWER LIMIT SWITCH  ON THE CONTROL PANEL KSR1 TURNING(817) TERMINAL AND B6 TERMINAL ON KTS CONNECT PARALLEL EACH OTHER . (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

14.  BRAKE BOBBIN OUT POINT (840-2000) PULL OUT ON THE CONTROL  PANEL C1 AND C4 TERMINALS ON KTS.  C2 AND C3 TERMINALS  ON KTS .CONNECT BRAKE BOBBIN OUTPUT TERMINAL (840-2000) ON THE CONTROL PANEL. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

15.  CONTROL PANEL`S 380V AC INPUTS (RST)AS  PARALLEL ON  KTS  D1,D2 AND D3 TERMINALS CONNECT CONTROL PANELS NOTR CONNECTION TO D4 TERMINAL ON KTS (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

16.  CONNECT ELEVATOR MOTOR HIGH SPEED TERMINALS ON CONTROL PANEL (U1,V1,W1) TO  E3,E4 AND E5 TERMINAL PARALLEL. (WITH 4 mm CABLE)

17.  IF THERE IS 3 PHASE AUTOMATIC DOOR TERMINALS (KU1,KV1,KW1)CONNECT  E6,E7 AND E8 TERMINALS ON THE KTS. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

18.  GROUND TERMINALS ON THE  CONTROL PANEL MAKE PARALLEL CONNECTION WITH E9 TERMINAL. (WITH 0,75 mm CABLE)

19.  CONNECT +POINT(RED)OF THE BATTERIES IN THE BATTERY BOX E2 TERMINAL ON THE KTS   ­--POINT(BLACK) E1 TERMINAL WITH THE CABLES GIVEN WITH KTS.

20.  AFTER CONTROLLING ALL CONNECTION LIFT UP W AUTOMATON.ON THE DISPLAY OF KTS   on IS WRITTEN. W AUTOMATON IS OPEN OR CLOSE NEITHER IT DON`T CHANGE CONTROL PANEL`S WORKING. FOR THE BEST WORKING FOR KTS MACHINE SHOULD BE IN on.

21.  AFTER MAKING KTS CONNECTIONS RUN AGAIN THE ELEVATOR AND MAKE SURE THERE

        IS NO ERROR.

22.  IF THERE IS NO PROBLEM WORKING GET THE ELEVATOR TO BETWEEN FLOOR  AND CUTOFF THE ELECTRIC AND MAKE SURE THAT KTS PUT INTO USE.

23.  WHEN KTS PUT INTO USE LEARN WHICH WAY IT TRIES.IF FIRST WAY IS UP  MAKE 14.PARAMETER “1”.IF NOT YOU SHOULD NOT HAVE TO CHANGE THE PARAMETERS.

24.  IN CASE OF FIRST FAILURE FOR KTS TO TURN AND TRY FOR OTHER DIRECTION NUMBER 6 TRIMMER SHOULD BE SET.  FOR SAFETY WHEN KTS COMES IN CIRCUIT, MOTOR MUST BE HOLD FROM FLYWHEEL AND GIVE REISTANCE TO MOTOR TO BE TURN AND TRIMMER SHOULD BE TURN TO CLOCKWISE DIRECTION UNTIL IT SHOWS Er7 ERROR MESSAGE ON THE KTS DISPLAY PANNEL.  WHEN ERROR DISPLAYED ON THE DISPLAY SCREEN, IT SHOULD SET AGAIN BY TURNING QUATER CYCLE IN ANTI-CLOCKWISE DIRECTION

NOTE :IF , WHEN KTS ENTER INTO ITS CIRCUITRY AND AFTER COMPLETING ITS TORQUE FOR MOTOR, GIVE Er7 ERROR MESSAGE AND TURN INTO OTHER DIRECTION, IT MEANS THAT SETTING FOR TURN HAS NOT BEEN SETTED CORRECTLY.  IF DISPLAY SHOWS Er7 ERROR MESSAGE WHILE MOTOR IS RUNNING, TRIMMER SHOULD BE TURN IN ANTI-CLOCK WISE DIRECTION WITH SMALL STEP.

 

27.  NECESSARY  SETTING FROM PROGRAMMING MODE FOR ELEVATOR MUST BE DONE ACCORDING TO ELEVATOR’S SITUATION.
