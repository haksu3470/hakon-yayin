PRINCIPLE OF WORK

 

Emergency rescue unit(KTS) works when electric cut off for the elevator system.  And stop the possiblity not to be stuck in the elevator.  When the electric shut off it gets the elevator nearest to the floor and if there is automatic door it opens the door and stop.

     KTS comes in use in case of any phases cut,  when elevator is between two floors and car door is close. When provisions come true  panels phase cut down by KTS.  At the end of the time which set on the surface of KTS opens the mechanic brakes produce 3 phases voltage gives motor a way and one way movement tested.  If it can’t turn that way it stops, wait 3 seconds and tried other way.  In these tries elevator must move to a way. If in all tries elevator don`t move, then there is mechanic tightness, brake bobbin or motor breakdown.  In this position KTS gives panel’s phase and quit from the circuit.

If the door opens when moving to any direction (no security)  movement, it quickly stop at that time and wait about 10 sec. The door closes.

All outputs of KTS’s has short circuit security. If there is short circuit in the brake, motor or nominal current over it has shown as error in the indicators.    It will show the same error when electricity come.   It will not come into service without deleting the error  even if the electric is turned off and on again.  To delete error message and to turn the KTS to off position up arrow button along with enter button must be press and hold for 3 seconds.  To turn it on again,  same buttons must be used for 3 seconds.
