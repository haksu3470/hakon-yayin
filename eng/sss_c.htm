What is the KTS ?

An abbreviation of the Floor Completing System is “KTS”, which is an apparatus and be programmed with the advanced technology, and it possesses a lightening panel interactive with the user.

 

What are the differences between the apparatus and the rescuers formed by cards?

At present, in the elevators, the compliance with IEC or CENELEC standards has become a compulsory act, and the apparatus providing these standards are being documented.

The documentation of the card types of the rescuers used in the elevators is impossible.

In case of an earthquake breaking-outs, if the building not flawed,

While the elevator moving, if electricity off

While the elevator moving, if one of the phases is cut off

While the elevator moving, if the network tension gets extremely up or down, for this reason, if the thermal control automatons are released. *(optional)

Your elevator with the KTS system is taken up to the nearest floor, and the trapped dwellers inside the cabin are rescued.

 

How does the KTS System work?

 

The elevator works in compliance with its control system, and in case of power interruption the KTS automatically gets into action. The cabin from its stuck place starts to move towards the nearest up or down floors, stops at floor level and the doors open. The rescue operation is completed. After rescuing, the electricity is expected to be on. When the electricity is on, the elevator turns back to the normal working system. During awaiting, the KTS makes itself off until the electricity on, and it does not spend any energy from the batteries.

 

Is the KTS system compatible with all of the elevator panels, doors and security circuits? Is there any necessary needs to the additional properties or changes in the elevator panel?

 

The KTS System is compatible with all of the standardized elevator panels, doors and security circuits. There is no need to put on any additional property and to make any changes in the elevator panel. Except the known, the technical supports for the door back-ups and control types are provided by the HAKON.

 

How does the KTS System determine the stuck place of the elevator cabin.

 

In the standardized elevator systems, the shaft (or pit) signaling is controlled with KSR1, KSR2, JF sensors.

 

How longest distance can the KTS work between the two floors?

 

The cabin to be taken by the KTS system to how far distances between the two floors are completely dependent upon the time schedule programmed. This distance may vary according to the given velocity of the elevator and the programmed time span.

In order to rescue during the electricity off, it is assumed that the emptied battery could be not recharged between these interruptions. In this case, the distance between the two floor is assumed to be the three meters, and the conclusion is that the cabin moves at least the nine to ten meters without stopping. This distance, mainly depending on the load, is longer in practice.

 

For the longer course, the battery bunches with the greater ampere-hour capacity are used. For this requirement, the technical support is provided by the HAKON.

What is the speed of the elevator cabin during the rescue operation?

 

Because of being the KTS speed programmable, during the rescue the cabin speed, as it is the inventory elevators, initially raises slowly then after increases up to the programmed peak limit, which is about one-twentieth of the nominal cabin speed.

 

While the KTS System was continuing to rescue operation, if the electricity was on again what would happen?

While the KTS System is continuing to rescue operation, if the electricity is on again, it continues to works as if the electricity is not on. After having completed the rescue operation, the elevator controller is transferred to its own panel (main panel).

 

In the KTS System, while overhauling of the elevator was being carried on, if the electricity was off, what would happen?

 

This situation causes that is the technician as dealing with the overhauling works on the cabin, may come across with the life threatening danger posed by “the other rescue systems”.

Whereas, while the overhauling is continuing on the elevator, the KTS System thanks to its revision controller does not do the rescue and does not cause an accident. link revision.

 

When the electricity is cut off, in the elevator installed place, if there is a generator as well, dose the KTS System do the rescue operation?

 

In the KTS System, the circuit insertion time can be selected as to be the three or 99 seconds. In case of a generator existence, if the circuit insertion time of the KTS is selected as the 10 seconds, within this time span if the generator supplies the electricity, then the KTS does not insert into the circuit, otherwise it does.

 

When the electricity is cut off, the KTS does insert into the circuit and does complete the rescue mission, does it redo its rescue operation soon after the power on and off again?

In case, the short intervals of the repeated electricity off and on again after a rescue operation, despite the battery are not sufficiently recharged, thanks to the technology used, it can repeatedly do the successive twenty rescues, at least.

Yes, it can.

 

In an apartment building, can the two elevators be commanded by a sole KTS System?

No, it can not. Each elevator cabin can only be controlled by a sole KTS System.

In an elevator breakdown, does the KTS System insert into the circuit too?

 

The KTS System, necessitating with the related instructions before the move, scrutinizes all the security contacts and if all are closed then it inserts into the circuit. The KTS System through inserting into circuit does not do the rescue in such cases as there might happen to the failures of the security contacts and breakdowns of the moving apparatus in the elevator. However, in case of some electrical failures of the main panel, if these breakdowns through a suitable contact combination are transferred into the KTS, the rescue operation can be done. Relating to these subjects, you should confer with the HAKON.

 

The batteries used in the KTS System and discharged during the rescue operation are they rechargeable? How long does their recharging time take?

 

As long as the electricity is on, the KTS System is recharged with the limited current and a tension self-adjusting to the environmental conditions. This kind of charging method is the most optimum (or appropriate) form for such an application. The charging time is the ten hours at maximum.

 

How long is the average life span of the batteries used in the KTS System?

In the KTS System, the battery type with unkempt, leaded-acid is used. These batteries in the state of the plugged charge and at 20 0C room temperature have the three to five years of life span, nevertheless their own catalogues notifies that the genuine life time, depending on the ambience temperature and the deepness and numbers of recharging, is halved. HAKON applies the best charging method recommended by the battery manufacturing firms so as to have the most productive and durable batteries in hand.

 

If the apparatus is awaited without inserting into the circuit, does the batteries discharge? How long does the discharge take? Is there a necessary treatment to the batteries?

 

In general, the batteries lose the charge as waiting. This event is very closely associated with the ambience temperature, again. The batteries, in such case of not being inserted into circuit, should be exposed to the recharging treatment. During the awaiting process, the ambiance temperature is recommended to be below 25 0C. Otherwise, they should be charged at more frequency intervals.

 

After having inserted the KTS System into circuit and completed the rescue operation, as waiting for the electricity on, is any electrical current receded from the batteries?

Not. The electrical current is not receded from the batteries.

In any elevator system, owing to the engine breakdown if the thermic is released, how does the KTS System act and does it cause a breakdown?

The reason for releasing of the thermic magnetic switch by the elevator engine is either to recede an excessive current by the engine or to confront with the phase by the control panel. In both cases, the thermic has done its function. As a result of that, if the elevator is stuck between the floors the KTS System will do the rescue operation, but if the engine recedes an excessive current from the KTS System, the KTS, in order to protect itself the warning made as “the ENGINE RECEDING EXCESSIVE CHARGE”, will not do the rescue maneuver.
