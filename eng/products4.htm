EARTHQUAKE SWITCH
DEPSEN switches the moving elevator to earthquake mode, as it senses the earthquake
DEPSEN activates the emergency rescue system for the evacuation of the elevator, which was stopped during the earthquake.
DEPSEN is a device that is designed to obstruct the hazards which will be able after earthquake in the elevator.
DEPSEN is a single-pole, double-throw svvitch, with one normally öpen contacts, one normally closed contacts, and one ground contacts.

Connect conduit and wire per applicable code requirements. DEPSEN must be manually reset as figures.

• Between 0.20 g and 0.40 g horizontal acceleration at 5 CPS;

Below 0.70 g horizontal acceleration at 7.5 CPS; &
